I come from a background in engineering, 3D art and digital production, and my work has taken me across different creative disciplines and environments. This experience has shaped how I approach ideas, combining technical thinking, visual exploration and practical problem-solving.

I am building North Stardust as an independent creative studio focused on exploring ideas, giving them structure, direction and room to grow. Ideas can evolve in different ways, taking form as creative works, experiences, tools, stories or something entirely new.

Looking ahead, I aim to build a sustainable studio that can grow beyond a single medium, develop original work, reach new audiences and create lasting value over time.
