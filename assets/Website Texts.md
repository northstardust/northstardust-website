## The Studio

North Stardust is an independent creative studio and a home for original Intellectual Property. It is built around the belief that good creative work needs both freedom and direction.

An idea is treated as raw material. It can be explored, tested, refined and given structure without losing the curiosity that made it worth pursuing. The studio values thoughtful making over constant output, and lasting value over quick results.

Its work can take many forms, from a useful digital tool or publication to an original character, story or world. Each begins with an idea and develops according to what it can become, guided by curiosity, purpose and a long-term view.

The North Star is not the destination. It is a point of reference that helps keep the work moving in the right direction.

---

## The Compass

To give curiosity a purpose and imagination a form.

The purpose is to explore ideas, shape them with intention and discover what they can become. A thought may grow into a useful tool, a resource, a creative work or the beginning of something larger.

The aim is to give promising ideas the space, structure and care they need to become meaningful and lasting work.

---

## The Horizon

To build a lasting creative practice where original ideas can grow into meaningful work, useful products and owned Intellectual Property.

Over time, the aim is to develop a diverse body of work across digital creation, publishing, worldbuilding and original IP, allowing each idea to evolve according to its potential.

The future is not defined by a single medium or outcome. It is about creating work with enough substance to stand on its own, connect with other work and continue to grow.

## Athanasios Zagkliveris

## FOUNDER & CREATIVE DIRECTOR

He brings together a background shaped by engineering, 3D art, digital production and creative development. His path has moved across different disciplines and creative environments, building a way of thinking that combines technical understanding with visual curiosity, problem-solving and a strong interest in how ideas are developed and brought to life.

Today, he leads North Stardust as an independent creative space focused on original Intellectual Property, worldbuilding, characters, stories and digital creative work. The studio gives him the freedom to explore ideas from their earliest stages, develop them through structured creative processes, and turn them into lasting creative assets rather than one-off projects.

Looking ahead, his aim is to grow North Stardust into a home for original worlds and ideas that can evolve over time. Through experimentation, documentation, publishing and collaboration, the studio is being built to support creative work that can move beyond a single medium and continue to develop, reach new audiences and create value for years to come.
